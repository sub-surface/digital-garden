## How to Use This Site

- **Wander**: <span className="on-desktop">links open notes beside the one you're reading, so you can follow a thought without losing your place. Hover a link to peek at it first.</span><span className="on-touch">tap a link to open its note. On a bigger screen, links open beside the note you're reading, and hovering one peeks at it first.</span>

- **The prompt**: <span className="on-desktop">press `Ctrl P` (or `/`) to open the prompt on the right.</span><span className="on-touch">tap › (where you are) in the bar at the bottom.</span> Type anything to search the whole garden, or try a command: `random`, `here`, `toc`, `thread`, `trail`, `ponder`, `sky`, `glorp`. <span className="on-desktop">`Tab` completes, `help` lists everything, and `?` shows the keyboard shortcuts.</span><span className="on-touch">`help` lists everything.</span>

- **Play some music while you browse**: <span className="on-desktop">press `M` to play or pause, or [start with this track](music:Eden). The turntable opens from the bar at the top.</span><span className="on-touch">tap ♫ in the bottom bar, or [start with this track](music:Eden). Tap the track's name for the turntable.</span>

- **Reading**: <span className="on-desktop">press `F` for reader mode, which hides everything but the text; `-` and `+` change the size, `[` and `]` the line length.</span><span className="on-touch">the book in the bottom bar hides everything but the text; the bar then holds A− and A+ for the size, and × reader to leave.</span>

- **Settings**: <span className="on-desktop">press `\` (backslash) for the theme, accent colour, reading and background (`B` cycles backgrounds).</span><span className="on-touch">··· → settings in the bottom bar has the theme, accent colour and reading options.</span> The sky lives there too: seasonal flurries, light that follows the sun, and live weather.

- **Chat**: <span className="on-desktop">press `C` to open philchat beside the note you're reading.</span><span className="on-touch">··· → philchat in the bottom bar.</span>

- **The other rooms**: the [wiki](https://wiki.subsurfaces.net), the [OS](https://os.subsurfaces.net) and the [arcade](/arcade) are linked <span className="on-desktop">at the top left</span><span className="on-touch">under ··· in the bottom bar</span>.

- **What's changed**: every change to the site is in the [commit history](https://github.com/sub-surface/digital-garden/commits/master). <span className="on-desktop">The short code at the top right is the commit you're looking at.</span>

- **About the site**: This [digital garden](https://github.com/sub-surface/digital-garden) began as a rebuild of [Quartz](https://quartz.jzhao.xyz/), a static site generator for digital gardens, and is now its own thing, written in React with Claude. The notes are written in Obsidian and turned into pages when the site is built.
