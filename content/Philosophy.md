Look, I know what you're here for, and no, I wont be able to do it justice.  

So, in light of that, here's some quotes to settle your mind:

> "Philosophy is a battle against the bewitchment of our intelligence by means of language."
> —Ludwig Wittgenstein, *Philosophical Investigations, §109*

> "that by which the given is given, that by which it changes."
> —Gilles Deleuze, *Difference and Repetition*

> "Sapere aude! Dare to know!"
> —Immanuel Kant, *Beantwortung der Frage: Was ist Aufklärung?*

> "The real is rational and the rational is real."
> —Georg Wilhelm Friedrich Hegel, *Preface to the Philosophy of Right*

> "God is dead. God remains dead. And we have killed him."
> —Friedrich Nietzsche, *The Gay Science, §125*

> "Language is the house of being."
> —Martin Heidegger, *On the Way to Language*

> "I am, I exist, is necessarily true whenever it is put forward by me or conceived in my mind."
> —Baruch Spinoza, *Ethics*

> "Cogito, ergo sum."
> —René Descartes, *Discourse on Method*

Don't forget to remember.
