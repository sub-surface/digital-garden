# dot's Profile

<div className="dropcap">

**dot** (operating under the handle `terrapin.station.`) sits firmly on the Spinozist side of Phil Chat alongside [[hughchungus|Hugh Chungus]]. Champion of non-linear dynamics, advocate of accessible no-code tooling, and resident auditor of server block lists, dot authored the celebrated "she kadys" formulation of diachronic personal identity. Despite his wry insistence that *"i don't read philosophy"*, his Spinozist intuitions and recommended texts (most notably Manuel DeLanda) routinely trigger multi-hour metaphysical disputes.

> *"dot's theory of personal identity, produced under pressure, is genuinely the best thing he said all week and we would like it entered into the philosophical record: 'but if kady got an alt — yes i would notice — bc kady is kady — she kadys.'"*  
> — The Phil Chat Times, Vol I No 5

</div>

## Known For

### The DeLanda Bookmark
In Vol I No 5, dot recommended Manuel DeLanda's *A Thousand Years of Nonlinear History* to [[myname1|My Name]]. Rather than keeping a reading list, My Name placed dot on Discord block so that every time dot's username appeared greyed out in chat, it would serve as an active reminder to read the book:

> *"Blocked until i read delaminate or whatever their name was."*

Dot's reaction at 1:53 A.M., upon discovering he had been converted into an operating-system filing widget, became channel folklore:

> *"Can u finish the book faster so u can unblock me I'd like to react to the badass messages like this with the apt emojis... But I fucking cant... Goddamnit fuck u fuck u."*

When informed of his status, dot gracefully accepted his fate: *"I'll miss u myname."*

### The Antigravity Demonstration
During the development debates in Vol I No 5 over how newcomers should build web applications, dot waged a solitary campaign against the terminal purists (who demanded raw git, Python, and Claude Code):

> *"ther is no doubt that yall are better coders than me. There is also no doubt that I would be the most help to her since I CANNOT CODE and i could get antigrav to do exactly what i want."*

He offered a live prizefight demonstration: *"I'm about to do this on antigrav and show u the finished website in 30 seconds u ready?"* Following an eight-minute pause, he directed the audience to direct messages. As *The Times* noted, whether the thirty-second website existed was not knowable from the public floor.

### The "She Kadys" Theory of Identity
On Friday night of Vol I No 5, dot prosecuted a two-hour case alleging that newcomer [[lizzie|Lizzie]] was an alt account of former resident Kady on grounds of *"identical mien"* and typing rhythm.

When challenged by [[janne|Janne]] on how someone could possess such acute person-blindness, dot delivered his immortal thesis on natural kinds and personal persistence:

> *"but if kady got an alt — yes i would notice — bc kady is kady — she kadys"*

When [[stackhouse|Stackhouse]] suggested a pharmacological transition (*"what if this was just kady after finally hopping on a ssri"*), dot accepted the model (*"yes ssri kady, the diachronic movement is smooooth"*), before adjusting the prescription to lithium upon consultation with Janne.

### The Oregon Coast Wire
During a transatlantic dispute over international beach quality with Monkey, in which Brazilian sands were proclaimed superior to all North American shores, dot retaliated with six photographs of the Oregon coast. When critics claimed his beaches hosted *"nasty disease homeless dogs"*, dot delivered the closing geopolitical verdict: *"Brazil would be nothing without Portugal."*

### The Block-Ledger Audit
Dot serves as the channel's chief forensic accountant regarding [[Quigley|Quigley's]] blocked accounts. When Quigley claimed to have initiated a universal amnesty, dot entered the receipts:

> *"I've been blocked for months just like the rest of chat. Would've been idk a month or two ago he verifiably had Charlie, Hugh, Jere, monkey, jeh, me, Don, synth, etc blocked, which is basically all the people that care enough to tell him his off putting."*
