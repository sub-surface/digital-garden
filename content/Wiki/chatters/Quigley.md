# Quigley's Profile

<div className="dropcap">

**Quigley** (Anthony) is a K-12 teacher in Massachusetts — a state he chose, by his own account, because it has the best public-school outcomes in the country and "if I want to get the best understanding of what works and what doesn't then I should be in the state that does the best." He works on educational reform along Deweyan lines and is, the paper holds, the most sincere person in the room. He is also the only chatter with a condition named after him.

> *Quigley* is "the most sincere person in this room and it is the reason everyone hates him."
> — The Phil Chat Times

</div>

## Known for

- **[[Warranted Assertibility|Warranted assertibility]].** The position he has defended, daily, across most of the paper's run — deflationary about "truth" as correspondence, pragmatic about what replaces it. His summary, delivered after a four-day bout: *"I've been arguing for Warranted Assertibility the whole time."* See [[The Warranted Assertibility Wars|the bout]].
- **[[The Six Criteria of Warrant|The six criteria of warrant]].** His working test for when an assertion is warranted, posted mid-bout and cited as precedent since.
- **[[Quigley Derangement Syndrome]].** The room's documented condition about him: he is answered more carefully than anyone and rewarded less. Vol VI: answered 78.4 times per hundred messages, the highest figure in the room; reaction percentile 26. *"Everybody answers him. Nobody laughs."*

## Positions on record

* **Truth:** Epistemic / warranted assertibility (anti-correspondence). "This. Is. Not. A. Theory. Of. Truth."
* **Education:** A fundamentally social phenomenon — "the biggest predictor of student success is the teacher-student relationship." Against auto-didacts: "Auto-didact is the educational equivalent of the Great Man Theory of History." Methodologically conservative inside the field (phonics over whole-word).
* **AI in the classroom:** Neoluddite by his own label; a model "cannot take on the social element." Coined "AI slopulism" for the superintelligence discourse.
* **Politics:** Self-described institutionalist — "liberal institutions" — in a room that despises them. Defends reparations with the definition sourced to the ICTJ.
* **Method:** Wants a procedure for everything: "how, step by step, does one recognise agency in the thing in front of one?"

## The god machine

His signature move, and the paper's, is the appeal to an adjudicator. In Vol IV he addressed a numbered summary of a disagreement *to* a language model — "To sum up our disagreement, God Machine" — named it Omnius, and submitted to its jurisdiction. When the transcript was fed back with an instruction to declare a winner, it ruled against him. By Saturday he had annexed the court: **"I am the new Omnius."**

## Voice

- "It's a shame you're unable to eat a cheeseburger without first knowing if it runs parallel to reality."
- "You must not be familiar with my Ethnocentrism."
- On the whole affair, in two words: **"Except I won."**
