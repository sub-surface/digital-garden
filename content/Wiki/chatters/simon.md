# Simon's Profile

<div className="dropcap">

**Simon** is a resident textual critic, political observer, and careful reader in Phil Chat. While participating across political economy and ontology threads, Simon earned a permanent place in the channel's literary lore during Vol I No 2 through his editorial treatment of [[don|Don]]'s declaration of affection.

> *"Simon repaired the word to 'not,' while My Name deleted it outright... Reverence transmits a text intact; wit cannot leave one alone, even when it cannot decide what is wrong with it."*
> &mdash; The Phil Chat Times, Vol I No 2

## Known for

- **The Textual Emendation.** When Don posted *"I have no appreciated you enough"* and five men repeated the typographical error character-for-character as an act of liturgical reverence, Simon could not abide the grammatical flaw. He filed a critical edition repairing *"no"* to *"not"*, prompting *The Times* to designate him the room's chief philologist.
- **Political Economy.** Regularly contributes nuanced structural analyses to debates on fiscal policy, wage labour, and institutional decay.

## Positions on record

* **Philology & Truth:** Holds that grammatical precision and lexical accuracy are essential preconditions for philosophical clarity; careless transcription corrupts meaning.
* **Dialectics:** Prefers reasoned textual critique over memetic amplification.
