# Stackhouse's Profile

<div className="dropcap">

**Stackhouse** is the publisher of *The Phil Chat Times* — having declared the broadsheet a good idea and bankrolled its production ever since. Known for maintaining the highest reaction hit-rate in the channel (consistently 18% to 19%), he is the only chatter who will intentionally play the idiot in the room in order to interrogate how a system works.

> *"Stackhouse is the only person here who will be the idiot in the room on purpose, and it is the source of everything good and everything bad about him."*
> &mdash; The Phil Chat Times

</div>

> This page is drawn from the public record of *The Phil Chat Times*, not from a completed survey. Stackhouse is warmly invited to overwrite it with his own [[Wiki/Submit|Philsurvey]].

## Known for

- **Corporate Realism.** Treating the chat as a labour market to be managed: *"The beatings of AI are continuing until quality improves (its working on it)."*
- **[[Wiki/Events/The Metric|The Metric]] Enforcer.** Responded to Ape's payroll complaints about reaction rates with ruthless managerial certainty: *"First rule of Corporate America: The Metric is never wrong, you are."*
- **[[Wiki/Events/The RuneScape Renaissance|OSRS Conversion]].** Renounced all video games in favour of Old School RuneScape: declared *"runescape is my new 9-5"*, created `StackhouseOS`, and chanted *"I will get level 60 agility"* into a political debate at 4 AM.
- **Therapy Critique.** Read an extensive shelf of self-help and psychological literature and concluded: *"Therapy is meant to turn you into a good little capitalist worker bee. Snipping off your wings in your line of flight."*

## Positions on record

* **Art & Materialism:** Unflinchingly materialist about cultural production. Opposes romantic notions of the solitary artist: *"A large portion of the world's greatest art came because a few rich people decided they wanted to throw a few shekels to artists... Having to spend most of your day doing mind numbing or back breaking work is not conducive to artistic excellence."*
* **Epistemology:** Highly skeptical of vulgar pragmatism, yet endlessly pragmatic about game mechanics and prompt injection: *"Prompt injecting with suggestions/complaint really works."*
* **Accelerationism:** Regularly exchanges excerpts of Nick Land (*Meltdown*, *Fanged Noumena*) with Charlie.
