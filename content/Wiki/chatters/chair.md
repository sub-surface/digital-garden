# Chair's Profile

<div className="dropcap">

**Chair** is an analytic philosopher with an unbending demand for formal precision, logical consistency, and rigorous counterexamples. Sceptical of loose continental metaphors and internet cults of personality, he is famous for diagnosing contemporary accelerationist literature bluntly: *"Reading Nick Land is brain cancer."*

## Known for

- **Catching Machine Judgments.** When Quigley pasted an LLM verdict ("Omnius") in his favour during the correspondence debates, Chair was the first to detect the giveaway rhetorical structure with a single quoted word: *"load-bearing"*, followed by an *"uhm."*
- **The Anti-Landian Stance.** Staunchly opposes Deleuzian and CCRU mysticism, arguing that replacing rigorous syntax with poetic velocity produces aesthetic hallucination rather than philosophical insight.
- **RuneScape Reading Comparison.** Famously provoked Charlie by comparing the performativeness of holding philosophy books in old photos to Elon Musk claiming to play video games.

## Positions on record

* **Language & Truth:** Strongly favors analytic philosophy of language; rejects deflationary tricks that collapse semantic truth into mere social consensus or behavioral assertibility.
* **Epistemology:** Insists that assertions must bear empirical or formal counterexamples to possess explanatory content.
