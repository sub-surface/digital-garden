# The Evolution Deliberation

The founding front-page headline of **Vol I No 1** (16 July 2026): a four-hour marathon inquiry into whether artificial intelligence models evolve and descend from humanity, concluding with zero consensus, three consecutive uses of the word *"erm"*, and the channel's inaugural yellow card.

<Clipping cite="The Phil Chat Times" issue="Vol I No 1, p. 1">
What began Thursday afternoon as a routine shitpost about whether the clankers are our children escalated within minutes into a four-hour engagement over the word “evolution.”
</Clipping>

## The Battle Lines

The engagement opened at 15:27 when Theophantism (Hugh) claimed that economic compute selection mirrors natural selection, making frontier models family:

> *"They are part of our family bro they're our children. But they will have their own soon enough."*

[[aurasurfer|aura]] immediately rejected the delivery, auditing the biological analogy: what is the hereditary mechanism? Where is the reproduction? Are humans selecting agents, or merely boosting cluster compute and claiming credit for evolution?

[[janne|Janne]] intervened with the institutional compromise: heredity in artificial systems does not live within model weights, but in accumulated human knowledge: *"you're not going to get very far if you only look at animals in the zoo."*

## The "Erm" Triad

<Tape label="15:38 UTC — The dialectical summit" />

Presented with a standard modern synthesis definition of evolution as statistical variation without teleological intent, the opposition responded in full:

- **15:38:** aura: *"erm"*
- **15:39:** [[jere|Jere]]: *"erm"*
- **15:41:** Hugh: *"erm"*

Hugh added: *"wow great argument aura"*—the only statement of the afternoon on which all participants agreed.

## The Turbo Objection & Symmetries

The dissident wing was anchored by Rn!, who deployed the engineering analogy that defined the opposition case:

> *"If I tune my car and add in a turbo it has.. evolved.."*

He escalated to a dictionary screenshot—in this channel, a formal warning shot across the bow. As *The Times* noted: *"He was never actually refuted — only outlasted, which in this channel is the same thing."*

Vannak attempted to elevate the technical ground, proposing that learning is nothing but the discovery of symmetries: if two systems find the same symmetry, the resemblance is not a metaphor but a physical fact about the world. He then noted that this is also why models struggle with arithmetic, which the room received as the afternoon's most devastating concession.

## The Machine Witness & The Yellow Card

At 16:01, the room resolved to summon an LLM to the stand: *"let's see what clank says."* (See [[The Machine Witness]]). The witness was read into the record at 16:05, testifying that frontier models undergo *"heredity of method, not heredity of material"*—comparing the process to a chef passing down a recipe rather than a biological parent transmitting DNA. 

Hugh promptly moved to impeach the witness: *"It conceded too easily smh."* He re-prompted it with Luna 5.6 post-training data until it concurred with his position, whereupon the chamber lost interest.

At 16:21, [[jere|Jere]] entered the historic disciplinary ruling:

## A Note on Method & Aftermath

At 16:48, with the engagement entering its fourth hour, a bystander asked what, exactly, everyone was doing. The answer was supplied at once: this was *folk discourse*, as opposed to the formal variety. The distinction was pronounced dead later that evening: *Cause of death: folk, ofc.*

The participants summarized their findings in order: *"erm"*; *"erm"*; *"erm"*; *"wow great argument aura"*; *"this is a boring conversation lmao"*; and *"i miss when this chat was just information, vague and underformed."*

The channel then spent the rest of the evening debating the Argentina-England World Cup semifinal. The issue price of Vol I was set at [[174 Million Tokens|174,000,000 tokens]].
