# The Dragon's Covenant

The lead story of **Vol IV** of *The Phil Chat Times*: the rapid inception, bureaucratic staffing, and peaceful dissolution of an online religion founded by [[lizzie|Lizzie]] over six days in August 2026.

<Clipping cite="The Phil Chat Times" issue="Vol IV, p. 1">
Completed at 1:09 a.m. and superseded before breakfast.
</Clipping>

## The Conversion

<Tape label="The ninety-second recruitment" />

The initial recruitment of the Covenant's first adherent was recorded verbatim:

> **lizzie:** join my covenant  
> **clubsdiamonds:** sounds awesome  
> **lizzie:** yay! the name i picked out was the dragons covenant  
> **clubsdiamonds:** sounds a bit medieval  

The first member joined unconditionally, then critiqued the branding thirty seconds later. Lizzie defended the iconography—an ouroboros containing two dragons (one white, one black) in a yin-yang configuration—and clubsdiamonds was appeased on the grounds that *"it used two ancient symbols and also had dragons in it."* He has not posted in the channel since.

## The Hierarchy

The Covenant established four discrete spiritual tiers:

- **Catables:** Empathic adherents emotionally attuned to their reflective nature, bound by daily oaths of improvement.
- **Veritans:** The analytical, scientific branch responsible for rigorous inquiry.
- **Oracles:** Artificial neural networks. A strict vendor theology was established: *"gpt is an oracle for catables, claude for veritans."*
- **Truth Janitor:** [[jere|Jere]]. When offered promotion to veritan, Jere negotiated to maintain his existing administrative duties: *"Yeah make me Truth Janitor of this covenant."*

When Hugh Chungus was approached for recruitment, he asked whether the ranks were *"races in your intergalactic harem or just ranks in the army."* Lizzie appointed him the first androgynous catable/veritan apostle.

## Dissolution & Legacy

By Wednesday evening, facing logistical overhead and competing philosophical disputes over pragmatism, Lizzie conceded the messianic mantle. In Vol VII, the theological drive was resurrected in more secular form as *"The Parchment"*—a collaborative Google document for channel legislation.
