# The Moggening

The lead story of **Vol I No 8** (2 September 2026): the week **zack** was called obese and spent thirty hours proving otherwise. It began mid-afternoon, inside a different argument, and ended with a moderator reaching for the ban button.

<Clipping cite="the front page" issue="Vol I No 8">
A man was called obese on Sunday afternoon. By Monday night he had measured a shin with a Porsche key, shaved one of his own legs, been served two rules, and lost the right to speak. The leg was rock solid throughout.
</Clipping>

## Sunday — the measuring

zack had spent an hour arguing that, since the state holds the monopoly on force, it is now more advantageous to be a woman. **Janne** suggested he try it; [[Quigley]] said he felt like part of zack's daily journal. Then FenceJumper delivered a two-word medical opinion — *"Zack is obese"* — and the afternoon changed subject.

What followed was a two-hour dispute over the *tibialis anterior*, the muscle at the front of the shin, conducted in the only unit to hand:

By eight the subject had moved down the body and off it — hairlines, then a garage of bicycles — and produced the week's other coinage, [[Spawnpill|the spawnpill]].

## Monday — the shave

<Tape label="one leg, one hour, no trimmer" />

Monday began with a foot, annotated (*extensor digitorum brevis*, with an arrow). It escalated to the exhibit that defined the volume: one leg shaved smooth to the knee, the other left as a control, because — zack explained — the hair had been *skin mogging* him, hiding the bigger muscle. He had no trimmer, so it took about an hour. His own caption, on discovering the colour underneath: *"it's been so long that i forgot shaving turns you Aryan."* He had assumed, until that moment, that he was sunburned.

FenceJumper filed the closing statement the paper took to the bank:

<Clipping cite="FenceJumper" issue="Vol I No 8, p. 3">
Zack I couldn't feel any less intimidated or "mogged" in my life. You're clearly in some sort of crisis state. If you can't help yourself, you should really find someone who can. Phil chat cannot.
</Clipping>

## The warning

Around four, **Pearl** told zack to chill on the leg fight. He called the ruling corrupt — *"Interesting. Compromised mod."* — and asked which rule he had broken. She sent two, and noted he was breaking the second as he asked. By late evening she had done what she said she had planned to do when she went to work. zack did not post on Tuesday. He did not post on Wednesday. On Tuesday afternoon [[myname1|My Name]] addressed the space where he had been: *"@zack you can talk about legs now."* He could not.

**Stackhouse**'s verdict stands as the week's: the leg mog off was the funniest thing to hit the chat in a while, and had made insecure a lot of people who cannot handle not being the centre of the room. FenceJumper's reply: *"Can't wait to read about it in the Times."*
