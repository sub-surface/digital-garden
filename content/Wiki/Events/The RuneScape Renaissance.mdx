# The RuneScape Renaissance

The sports and culture lead of **Vol IV** and **Vol V**: the sudden, sweeping return of Old School RuneScape (OSRS) to a channel that had spent the preceding six months arguing about Deleuzian difference and p-zombies.

<Clipping cite="The Phil Chat Times" issue="Vol IV, p. 7">
Mentions this week: RuneScape 17, Kant 17. We have counted twice.
</Clipping>

## The Confession

The renaissance began when [[Charlie(Willow)|Charlie]] was confronted with an old photograph of himself holding an open philosophy book with the caption *"are you reading babe &lt;3"*. Rather than defend his academic credentials, Charlie made the disclosure that governed the following fortnight:

> It is an old picture. He has not read in a long time. **He is a stupid chud addicted to RuneScape.**

[[chair]] immediately pointed out the comparison between the performativeness of holding philosophy books in photographs and tech billionaires claiming to play video games.

Moments later, Apustus arrived with the only question that matters: *"07 or 3?"* Satisfied that it was Old School, he declared himself an ironman and demanded Charlie's in-game account name:

> **"HegelBot."**

## The Box Score that Ended a War

<Tape label="Monday, 5:56 PM — The p-zombie armistice" />

On Monday afternoon, hour four of an acrimonious debate regarding whether phenomenal consciousness is logically supervenient on physical states, insults were flying between physicalists and dualists. [[stackhouse|Stackhouse]] abruptly halted the thread to ask Trader what his in-game skills were.

Trader posted a full-screen client capture:
- **Total level: 2,376.**
- A solid wall of level 99 skills the entire height of the interface.
- Combat boosted to 106.
- **Hitpoints: 50/99. Prayer points: 6.**

He was not posing in Lumbridge. He was mid-fight in an endgame encounter with six prayer points remaining, and had paused his rotation to answer a question about his stats in a philosophy server.

The room capitulated in unison. [[aurasurfer|aura]] delivered the verdict:

> *"imagine ur hiring a lawyer to save u from a life sentence and u find out he maxxed out his runescape character"*

[[stackhouse|Stackhouse]] converted on the spot, created `StackhouseOS`, and declared RuneScape his new full-time employment. On Thursday, into a heated war on Middle Eastern geopolitics at 3:50 AM, he contributed a single message:

> *"Why would you play any other game than OSRS"*

When informed that grinding level 60 Agility takes two days of casual clicking, he typed *"I will get level 60 agility"* four consecutive times without spaces, prompting Charlie to issue the immortal remote diagnostic: **"RuneLite not open."**

## Automation & Ethics

Charlie built an automated agent using `rs-sdk` that achieved *"100% success with tick-perfect prayer switching on a boss simulation in 20 minutes"* and reported it to the channel as an intellectual breakthrough. Nobody raised a technical objection.

The only moral objection came from Willow:

> *"Is it ethical to dox your other online spaces with your RSN?"*
