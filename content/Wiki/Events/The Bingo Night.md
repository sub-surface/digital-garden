The President delivered a hastily announced primetime address on Thursday night alleging that China had obtained the data of tens of millions of American voters. Phil Chat prepared for the historic moment in the traditional manner.

## The Cards

Two hours out, [[myname1|My Name]] announced a speech bingo and solicited vocabulary. [[hughchungus|Theophantism]] submitted "biden war inflation tremendous." [[don|El Don]] submitted "Raggedy ass bitch," then clarified that he had not understood the assignment. CronyKapitalist submitted "Iran" and, separately, "Billions Millions Trillions."

The cards were generated by machine and released to the public at 01:01.

> "fight over the cards mfs" -- My Name

El Don announced that, to spite the cardmaker, he would not use them. Quality control was, by the publisher's own admission, imperfect. "omg i put inflation in there twice lmao," My Name reported, three minutes into the broadcast. "fuck it millions."

By 01:05 the broadcast had been interrupted by what witnesses describe as a live plug for TRUMPRX.GOV, producing the evening's only unanimous verdict.

The substance -- a new election-integrity portal offering downloadable documents on data security and registration practices -- was analyzed by the method now standard in this channel. "yeah but we gave the ai the pdfs instead of reading it," My Name explained.

[[papagaio]], one of the few residents to open the documents himself, surfaced a 2013 C.I.A. "Devil's Advocacy" exercise concerning the 2012 Venezuelan election.

## The Unanswered Question

Angel, who had prepared two homemade burritos, raised the evening's outstanding question and received no answer:

> "So China got information of voter affiliation and behavior... What does that have to do with voter fraud."

Reached for comment on the falling of democracy, moderator [[pearl|Pearl]] said: "Again?" She added, on reflection: "Oh no. It's bingo time."

By 01:22 the President was, in the estimation of the press gallery, getting sleepy. By 01:28 it was over -- and one resident had bingo and did not know it.
