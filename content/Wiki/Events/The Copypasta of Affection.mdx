# The Copypasta of Affection

The lead story of **Vol I No 2** (23 July 2026): an undefended declaration of friendship posted into the middle of a debate on philosopher loneliness, repeated verbatim five times within fifteen minutes, and ruled sincere by *The Phil Chat Times*.

<Clipping cite="The Phil Chat Times" issue="Vol I No 2, p. 1">
There is no polite way for a man in this channel to say that he is fond of the other men in it, and shortly after midday on Thursday one of them tried anyway.
</Clipping>

## The Incident

Shortly after noon on Thursday, in the midst of a sharp dispute over whether philosophers are lonelier than other men, [[don|Don]] posted a 64-word reflection:

> these people are my only friends, I have no appreciated you enough, and I hope it lasts.

The message carried a typo Don did not correct: *"I have no appreciated you enough."* It sat in the channel for approximately sixty seconds without a response.

## The Antiphonal Chain

<Tape label="The quarter-hour of sincerity" />

Rather than deriding the vulnerability, [[Charlie(Willow)|Charlie]] posted the message back at Don. Not as a reply—as an exact, verbatim reproduction.

Within the minute, [[ice_chicken|Jeh]] did the same. [[stackhouse|Stackhouse]] followed two minutes later. After a ten-minute pause, Hugh Chungus revived the passage, and [[sizzurp1|Sizzurp]] closed it. Six identical paragraphs of undefended affection stood in the transcript across fifteen minutes.

*The Times* recorded:

> *"Jeh and Stackhouse each spoke exactly once in eight hundred and sixty-six lines of Thursday, and that once was this."*

[[myname1|My Name]] inverted the paragraph into a declaration of enmity, wishing that the rivalry continue *"throughout the minutes"* and swapping shooting the shit for shooting the president. [[simon|Simon]] produced a critical edition correcting the grammar. But the original uncorrected chain remained the emotional high-water mark of the summer.
