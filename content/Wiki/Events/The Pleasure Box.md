At 15:11 on the first Thursday, moments after proposing that the elderly be uploaded into a less racist version of themselves, [[jere|j3r3j98f4u87fq9r3f0bx8r7c]] announced that he had an idea.

The idea: build a conscious box. Arbitrarily set it to maximal pleasure -- "like, just an unimaginable ecstasy." Every util you could ever generate out here is then dwarfed by the utils in there. Therefore, he concluded, "everything is permitted so long as that box stays online."

Peer review was immediate. "this is like a lesswrong post from 2004," said [[hughchungus|Theophantism]]. The author accepted the citation and offered his own: the exact opposite of the Le Guin, he said, of the child in the basement.

## The Ethics Board Objects

The ethics board, sitting as [[janne|Janne]], raised the standard objection.

> "isn't it easier to just do heroin?"

"nah," the author replied, "cuz heroin for one person doesn't make slavery and other evil ok."

Challenged on his standing to speak of maximum pleasure -- "dude you don't know pleasure" -- the author clarified the epistemics: "wrong, i've simply seen it."

## Deployment Refused

At 15:18 the author moved from theory to procurement. "im not kidding though, make the AI pleasure box. hugh, get on this."

Theophantism declined, citing a safety case.

> "ai will kill to join the pleasure box. officially, it cannot exist."

He then added: "unofficially... let's just say i book my thursday nights off work."

The author noted a bright side: once the box exists, paperclip maximization ceases to be a problem, on grounds of motivation. Janne noted a dark one: "all must be pleasure box."

## The Withdrawal

Sensing a stampede, the author moved to withdraw. "u kno i'm just, like, bullshit theorizing right. like, obviously I am not endorsing this. actually."

"why do you have to ruin it," said Janne.

Janne was thereupon admitted to the circle of trust, officially. The proposal was filed as "a utilitarian L -- or at least a quant util L."
