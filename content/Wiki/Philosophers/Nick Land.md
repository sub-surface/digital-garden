# Philosopher: Nick Land

> "Nothing human makes it out of the near-future."

## Quick Info
* **Dates:** 1962 – present
* **Nationality:** British
* **Philosophical Tradition:** Continental (post-Deleuzean)
* **Areas of Specialization (AOS):** Political economy, cybernetics, aesthetics
* **Notable For:** Accelerationism, hyperstition, the CCRU

---

## Biography Summary

<div className="dropcap">

Nick Land is a British philosopher whose early-1990s work at Warwick — and in the informal Cybernetic Culture Research Unit (CCRU) — fused Deleuze and Guattari, cybernetics, science fiction and political economy into a body of writing on capital as an autonomous, intelligence-producing process. His theoretical vocabulary (accelerationism, hyperstition) has since propagated well beyond academic philosophy. His later political writing is associated with the neoreactionary movement and is widely rejected; the philosophical and aesthetic ideas travel independently of it, which is roughly how Phil Chat handles him.

</div>

## Key Philosophical Contributions

### Accelerationism
The thesis, in its Landian form, that capitalism is best understood not as a system humans run but as a runaway feedback loop that leaves intelligence in its wake — and that the process, rather than being resisted, tends to intensify. Left and right variants disagree about what to do with the diagnosis. In Phil Chat, [[hughchungus|Hugh]]'s "cute accelerationism" is a deliberate answer to Land's "grungy techno-dystopia" — the same recognition of an autonomous process, redirected onto cuteness rather than dread.

### Hyperstition
Fictions that make themselves real: ideas which, by being circulated and acted upon, bring about the future they describe. A CCRU coinage that has outlived its origin.

## Influence & Legacy
* **Influenced by:** Deleuze & Guattari, Bataille, Kant (against), Blade-Runner-era SF
* **In Phil Chat:** the reference point for the room's accelerationist strand. Hugh's account of *Cute/Acc* — "written in direct opposition to the darkness of Land's sci-fi cyberpunk picture," recognising "the autonomy and intentionality of cute... as auto-catalytic process" — is the clearest local statement of what the room keeps and what it throws away.
