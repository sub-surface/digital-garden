# Frank Jackson

<div className="dropcap">

**Frank Cameron Jackson** (born 1943) is an Australian analytic philosopher and Distinguished Professor Emeritus at the Australian National University. Renowned worldwide for inventing the **Knowledge Argument** against physicalism—formulated via the celebrated thought experiment of [[Marys Room]][^jackson-qualia-ref]—Jackson has stood for decades at the center of contemporary debates regarding consciousness, [[Philosophical Zombies]], and the status of conceptual analysis.

</div>

In an intellectual evolution rare among major philosophers, Jackson spent the 1980s as the foremost champion of dualist epiphenomenal qualia, before later converting to a sophisticated form of reductive **Type-A physicalism** (the "Canberra Plan"), arguing that his own famous knowledge argument must contain an elusive flaw.

---

## 1. The Knowledge Argument and Mary's Room

In his landmark 1982 paper *"Epiphenomenal Qualia"*[^jackson-qualia-ref], Jackson formulated an intuitive, elegant thought experiment designed to demonstrate that **Physicalism is incomplete**:

```
                         THE MARY SCENARIO
       ┌─────────────────────────────────────────────────────┐
       │   MARY IN THE BLACK-AND-WHITE ROOM                  │
       │   - Knows ALL physical facts about vision           │
       │   - Understands wave mechanics, retinal chemistry,  │
       │     optic nerve transmission, and visual cortex     │
       └──────────────────────────┬──────────────────────────┘
                                  │
                                  ▼  RELEASED INTO THE WORLD
       ┌─────────────────────────────────────────────────────┐
       │   MARY SEES A RIPE RED TOMATO                       │
       │   Does she learn something new about the world?     │
       └──────────────────────────┬──────────────────────────┘
                                  │
             ┌────────────────────┴────────────────────┐
             │                                         │
             ▼                                         ▼
         YES, SHE DOES                            NO, SHE DOES NOT
   She learns what red FEELS like.             Physicalism remains intact;
   Physical information ≠ All info.            she merely acquired a new
   Qualia are real, non-physical.              representational ability
   (Dualism follows)                           (Lewis/Nemirow Ability Reply)
```

The argument proceeds through three concise premises:
1. Mary knows all the physical information there is to know about other people before she is released.
2. Upon being released and seeing a red rose or ripe tomato for the first time, Mary learns something new about the world (namely, *what it is like* to experience red).
3. Therefore, there is information about human experience that is not captured by physical information: **Physicalism is false**.

[^jackson-qualia-ref]: Frank Jackson, "Epiphenomenal Qualia," *The Philosophical Quarterly* 32, no. 127 (1982): 127–136. See [[Bibliography#Jackson-Qualia-1982]].

## 2. Epiphenomenalism

Having rejected physicalism in 1982, Jackson confronted the problem of mental causation. Rather than embracing Cartesian interactionist substance dualism (which violates the physical conservation of energy), Jackson defended **Epiphenomenalism**:
* Physical brain events cause subjective qualia.
* Qualia themselves possess **zero causal influence** on the physical world; they are causal by-products (*epiphenomena*), like the shadow cast by a moving locomotive or the steam emitted by a whistle.
* Jackson famously dismissed the objection that pain causes us to pull our hands away from fire: the physical damage in the hand causes *both* the muscle contraction *and* the conscious pain sensation, creating the illusion of mental causation.

## 3. The Great Recantation and the Canberra Plan

By the late 1990s, Jackson came to regard epiphenomenalism as an untenable worldview. In his 1998 John Locke Lectures, published as *From Metaphysics to Ethics: A Defence of Conceptual Analysis*, Jackson announced his conversion to **Physicalism**:

### The Canberra Plan
Together with David Lewis and Philip Pettit, Jackson developed the **Canberra Plan**:
1. **Conceptual Analysis (Ramsey-Lewis Method)**: We begin by assembling everyday folk concepts (folk psychology, folk morality) and identifying their functional, causal roles (*"pain is the state typically caused by bodily damage that causes distress and avoidance behavior"*).
2. **Empirical Location**: Empirical science (neurobiology, physics) then discovers which physical states actually realize or fulfill those functional roles in the actual world (e.g., C-fibers or thalamocortical loops).
3. If physical science accounts for all the causal interactions of the universe, then consciousness must simply be identical with those physical realizers.

Regarding Mary, the converted Jackson concluded that Mary does not acquire knowledge of non-physical facts; rather, her pre-release knowledge was incomplete in representational format, or she merely acquired new recognitional abilities (*The Ability Hypothesis* of David Lewis).
