## 1. Canonical Philosophical Concepts & Dilemmas

### Epistemology & Theory of Inquiry
* **[[Warranted Assertibility]]**: John Dewey's deflationary pragmatist successor to truth—propositions surviving rigorous communal inquiry.
* **[[The Six Criteria of Warrant]]**: The audit standards of inquiry: predictive power, explanatory power, internal consistency, coherence with web of belief, fruitfulness, and simplicity.
* **[[The Problem of Induction]]**: Hume's challenge to the rational justification of inferring unobserved regularities from past observation.
* **[[Foundationalism vs Coherentism]]**: Competing architectures for the justification of empirical and inferential beliefs.
* **[[Gettier Problems]]**: Counterexamples refuting the classical Justified True Belief (JTB) account of propositional knowledge.

### Mind, Cognition & Perception
* **[[The Hard Problem of Consciousness]]**: Explaining why and how physical computational processing generates phenomenal subjectivity (*qualia*).
* **[[Marys Room]]**: Frank Jackson's knowledge argument against physicalist theories of phenomenal experience.
* **[[The Chinese Room]]**: John Searle's demonstration that syntactic processing cannot produce semantic intentionality.
* **[[Philosophical Zombies]]**: Metaphysical conceivability of physical replicas devoid of conscious experience.
* **[[The Mind-Body Problem]]**: The ontological relation between mental events and neurobiological states.

### Metaphysics, Identity & Modality
* **[[The Ship of Theseus]]**: The paradox of persistence, material replacement, and identity over time.
* **[[Substance Monism]]**: Spinoza's metaphysics of a singular infinite substance (*Deus sive Natura*) expressing itself through infinite attributes.
* **[[Radical Immanence]]**: The rejection of transcendent theological or metaphysical causes in favor of internal causality.
* **[[The Becoming-Document]]**: Ontological tracking of reality as dynamic textual inscription and record.

### Ethics, Decision Theory & Value
* **[[Newcombs Problem]]**: The decision-theoretic dilemma between Causal Decision Theory (two-boxing) and Evidential Decision Theory (one-boxing).
* **[[The Trolley Problem]]**: Philippa Foot's classic scenario analyzing the distinction between killing and letting die.
* **[[The Experience Machine]]**: Robert Nozick's thought experiment contesting hedonistic accounts of well-being.
* **[[The Veil of Ignorance]]**: John Rawls' hypothetical contractarian device for determining impartial principles of justice.
* **[[The Is-Ought Problem]]**: Hume's demonstration of the logical breach between descriptive facts and normative obligations.

## 2. Philchat Dialectical Apparatus & Channel Folklore

The conceptual apparatus chronicled across the eight volumes of *The Phil Chat Times*:

### Jurisprudence & Procedural Audit
* **[[The Yellow Card]]**: An administrative booking issued for egregious self-contradiction, evasion, or bad faith procedural maneuvers.
* **[[The Machine Witness]]**: The procedural ritual of summoning a large language model to arbitrate human dispute, followed by mutual impeachment.
* **[[The Four-Word Ward]]**: The rhetorical lever *"what does this mean"*, used to purchase exhaustive paragraphs of explanation from academic debaters.
* **[[Quigley Derangement Syndrome]]**: Server exasperation provoked by the Deweyan immunity to classical correspondence refutation.

### Somatic & Reaction Scales
* **[[Size 2 Chuckle]]**: The standardized somatic reaction metric: an audible, nasal exhale registering amusement without derailing text flow.
* **[[The Moggening]]**: Caliper empiricism and bodily measurement disputes during the bicycle baseline saga of Volume VIII.
* **[[Spawnpill]]**: The fatalist thesis asserting that biological starting conditions dictate trajectory with mathematical finality.

### Liturgy & Channel Relics
* **[[Antiphonal Copypasta]]**: The liturgical verbatim repetition of a peer's sincere emotional vulnerability to transform awkwardness into communal solidarity.
* **[[The Tub]]**: Ten gallons of industrial lye in a basement plastic storage tub adjacent to a 15-quart pressure canner.
* **[[174 Million Tokens]]**: The exhaustion of monumental computational resources with zero net output (*"And I must token"*).
* **[[The Pleasure Box]]**: A speculative conscious entity set to maximal ecstasy, dwarfing all earthly utilitarian equations.
