**174 Million Tokens** refers to the legendary computational expenditure reported on 16 July 2026 (Vol I) by [[myname1|My Name]], who burned through 174,000,000 language model tokens between one sleep and the next while generating zero net production.

<Clipping cite="The Phil Chat Times" issue="Vol I No 1, p. 4">
"I managed to burn 174M tokens in a day. :Pog: I got nothing done. And I must token."
— My Name, 13:11 UTC
</Clipping>

## The Scale of Consumption
As calculated by the *Business & Markets* desk of *The Phil Chat Times*, 174 million tokens represents:
- Several entire civic libraries of text
- More words than an individual will read or speak in an entire human lifespan
- More vocabulary than exchanged across the entire historical archive of Phil Chat up to that date
- A 74% overrun above My Name's original target of 100 million tokens

Output for the period was independently audited and confirmed at zero.
