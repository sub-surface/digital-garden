# The Tub

<div className="dropcap">

**The Tub** is the standing, unresolved forensic mystery of *The Phil Chat Times*, spanning all published volumes. It concerns the unexplained presence, photographic documentation, and industrial stockpiling of chemical agents and pressure vessels by channel participants.

</div>

## The Artifacts

The dossier consists of two primary photographic exhibits submitted to the channel without context:

1. **Exhibit A (Vol II):** A high-density polyethylene storage tub containing ten gallons of technical-grade sodium hydroxide (lye), photographed by Brutus on a residential basement floor.
2. **Exhibit B (Vol IV):** A 15-quart commercial-grade All-American cast aluminum pressure canner, sitting unboxed on an adjacent work surface.

## Editorial Investigation

The editorial staff of *The Phil Chat Times* initially treated Exhibit A as an eccentric soap-making experiment. However, upon the arrival of Exhibit B two issues later, the paper entered the following official finding into the permanent record:

> *"We are no longer confident the two facts are unrelated."*
> &mdash; Vol IV, Police Blotter, p. 14

Repeated inquiries directed at Brutus during subsequent editions yielded only vague references to "homesteading", "lye soap", and "meat preservation". When pressed by [[Wiki/chatters/stackhouse|Stackhouse]] on whether the basement setup met local municipal hazardous waste codes, the conversation was abruptly redirected toward the ontological status of p-zombies.

In Vol VII's Classifieds section, the tub received its final public notice:
