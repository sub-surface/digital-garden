# Mogging

**To mog** (from "AMOG", *alpha male of the group*) is to outclass someone on a specific, usually measurable axis. It is Phil Chat's native unit of relative status, applied with total seriousness to things no status system has considered before: shin muscles, git commit counts, hairlines, the age of one's refrigerator. The passive — *to get mogged* — is the more common form.

<Tape label="mog or be mogged" />

## The theology

Left alone, mogging inflates into first principles. During [[The Moggening]], **Janne** pressed the concept to its limit and **zack** followed it there:

- *"aren't you being mogged by life right now?"*
- *"I mog billions of people."*
- *"yet you're mogged by life."*

The escalation ends where such things end. Everyone but a single supreme mogger is mogged to some degree, zack reasoned, and no one knows who that is: **"The best mogger next to god."** Janne's counsel — *"just don't be mogged"* — is the room's whole ethics in four words.

## Varieties attested

- **Leg mog / skin mog** — the tibialis dispute; "skin mogging" is winning on visible definition alone, which is why zack shaved one leg to level the field.
- **Git mog** — see [[Wiki/My-Git-Commits-Mog-Your-Git-Commits|My Git Commits Mog Your Git Commits]]; velocity as a social construct.
- **Muscle mog, hairline mog, bike mog** — all attested in a single Sunday.
