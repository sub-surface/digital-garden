# Bibliography & Citation Index

<div className="dropcap">

The **Bibliography & Citation Index** serves as the master bibliographic repository for the Philchat Wiki. It records canonical editions, standard academic pagination standards (Stephanus, Bekker, Akademie), and authoritative translations cited across wiki entries. 

</div>

Wiki articles cite sources in right-margin Tufte sidenotes using standard citation keys defined in this index. Use standard Obsidian wikilink syntax: `[[Bibliography#Key]]` or `[[Bibliography#Key|Author (Year)]]`. Hovering over any citation anchor displays an immediate bibliographic preview popover. See [[Citation Guide|The Wiki Citation Guide]] for formatting rules and guidelines on handling unsourced claims.
