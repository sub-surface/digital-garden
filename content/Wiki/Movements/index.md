## Chronological & Methodological Traditions

### 1. Ancient & Classical Traditions
* **[[Milesian Naturalism]]**: The search for a primary material substrate (*arche*) independent of mythological theology (Thales, Anaximander, Anaximenes).
* **[[Eleatic Monism]]**: Parmenidean deduction of immutable Being and the rejection of flux and non-being (Parmenides, Zeno of Elea).
* **[[Ancient Atomism]]**: Mechanistic cosmology of indivisible particles colliding in infinite void (Leucippus, Democritus, Lucretius).
* **[[Platonism]]**: The ontological primacy of the transcendent intelligible Forms over the sensible world of appearances.
* **[[Aristotelianism]]**: Hylomorphic metaphysics, teleology, category theory, and empirical biological observation.
* **[[Stoicism]]**: Materialist pantheism, cosmic logos, universal brotherhood, and virtue as the sole intrinsic good.
* **[[Epicureanism]]**: Atomistic physics paired with tranquil hedonism (*ataraxia* and *aponia*) free from superstitious terror.
* **[[Pyrrhonian Skepticism]]**: Systematic suspension of judgment (*epoche*) regarding dogmatic claims to achieve mental tranquility.
* **[[Neoplatonism]]**: Hierarchical emanation of reality from the transcendent One through Nous and World Soul (Plotinus, Proclus).

### 2. Early Modern & Enlightenment Lineages
* **[[Continental Rationalism]]**: Epistemological foundation in innate rational ideas and geometric deductive necessity (Descartes, Spinoza, Leibniz).
* **[[British Empiricism]]**: Grounding of all ideas in sensory impression, leading to critical boundaries on knowledge (Locke, Berkeley, Hume).
* **[[Kantian Critical Philosophy]]**: Transcendental synthesis of rationalism and empiricism—examining the a priori conditions of possible experience.

### 3. 19th-Century Developments
* **[[German Idealism]]**: Systematic post-Kantian absolute metaphysics reconciling subject and object through historical dialectic (Fichte, Schelling, Hegel).
* **[[Historical Materialism]]**: Marxist dialectical critique locating the driver of historical development in class struggle and relations of production.
* **[[Utilitarianism]]**: The consequentialist principle that actions are morally right in proportion as they tend to promote happiness or utility (Bentham, Mill).
* **[[Deontology]]**: Duty-based ethics grounding moral imperatives in rational autonomy and respect for persons (Kant, Ross, Scanlon).
* **[[Virtue Ethics]]**: Character-centered ethics emphasizing eudaimonia, practical wisdom, and human flourishing (Aristotle, Foot, MacIntyre).
* **[[Pragmatism]]**: The meaning and truth of ideas evaluated by their practical bearings and experimental survival (Peirce, James, Dewey).
* **[[19th-Century Existentialism]]**: Individual subjective existence, freedom, dread, and authenticity preceding essence (Kierkegaard, Nietzsche).

### 4. 20th & 21st-Century Analytic Currents
* **[[Logical Positivism]]**: Radical verificationism, elimination of metaphysics, and logical analysis of scientific language (Vienna Circle, Carnap).
* **[[Ordinary Language Philosophy]]**: Dissolving philosophical puzzles through careful attention to ordinary linguistic usage (Ryle, Austin, late Wittgenstein).
* **[[Analytic Naturalism & Physicalism]]**: The metaphysical commitment to a world exhaustively described by natural and physical sciences.
* **[[Epistemic Foundherentism]]**: Susan Haack's intermediate model reconciling foundational experiential evidence with coherentist mutual support.

### 5. 20th & 21st-Century Continental Currents
* **[[Phenomenology]]**: Rigorous description of the invariant structures of conscious experience from the first-person perspective (Husserl, Merleau-Ponty).
* **[[Fundamental Ontology & Existentialism]]**: Dasein's temporal being-in-the-world and radical human situated freedom (Heidegger, Sartre, Beauvoir).
* **[[Frankfurt School Critical Theory]]**: Interdisciplinary critique of capitalist domination, authoritarianism, and instrumental rationality (Adorno, Horkheimer, Habermas).
* **[[Post-Structuralism & Deconstruction]]**: Radical critique of stable origins, binary oppositions, and totalizing philosophical systems (Derrida, Foucault, Deleuze).
* **[[Accelerationism]]**: The technological and cybernetic acceleration of capitalist runaway deterritorialization (Nick Land, CCRU).

### 6. Eastern & Non-Western Traditions
* **[[Advaita Vedanta]]**: Non-dualist metaphysical system affirming the singular reality of Brahman and identity of the self (Shankara).
* **[[Madhyamaka]]**: The Buddhist "Middle Way" establishing the thoroughgoing emptiness (*sunyata*) of all phenomena (Nagarjuna).
* **[[Classical Daoism]]**: The effortless harmony of human agency with the unnamable, non-coercive Way (Laozi, Zhuangzi).
* **[[Confucianism]]**: Moral cultivation, relational ethics, and societal harmony through ritual propriety and humaneness.
