## Canonical Philosophers

Historical thinkers whose frameworks form the battlegrounds of the server:

* **[[John Dewey]]** — Pragmatism, instrumentalism, inquiry as organism-environment interaction.
* **[[Richard Rorty]]** — Anti-representationalism, truth as social practice, philosophy as conversation.
* **[[Baruch Spinoza]]** — Substance monism, radical immanence, affect and conatus.
* **[[Gilles Deleuze]]** — Difference in itself, lines of flight, *Logic of Sense*.
* **[[Susan Haack]]** — Foundherentism and the crossword puzzle model of justification.
* **[[Nick Land]]** — Accelerationism, teleoplexy, and the machinic unconscious.
