# About the Philchat Wiki

The Philchat Wiki is a collaborative knowledge base built by and for the philchat community — a group of philosophy enthusiasts who gather to discuss ideas, argue positions, and occasionally agree on things.

## How articles are structured

**Canonical text analyses** follow a strict seven-part structure: textual overview and context, formal architecture, core axioms and deductions, key arguments and landmark formulations, internal aporias, critical objections/reception, and bibliographic apparatus. See [[Ethics (Spinoza)]] or [[Critique of Pure Reason (Kant)]] for examples.

**Thought experiments & concepts** isolate a scenario, formulate its logical premises, analyze competing theoretical reactions, and reconstruct primary objections. See [[The Teletransporter]] or [[The Brain in a Vat]] for examples.

**Philosopher profiles** follow a standard format: a quick-info block, biography summary, key contributions, a PhilPapers-style survey, major works, and influence notes. See [[Baruch Spinoza]] for an example.

**Chatter profiles** use the Philsurvey format — a standardised set of questions drawn from the PhilPapers survey. See [[Ape|Ape's Profile]] for an example.

**Movement articles** document the historical genesis, foundational tenets, internal debates, and historical legacy of a tradition. See [[Deontology]] or [[Pragmatism]] for examples.

## Who maintains it

The wiki is maintained by Hugh Chungus (@hughchungus) with contributions from the community. It's built on a custom CMS running on Cloudflare Workers, with content stored as Markdown files in a GitHub repository.

---

## Technical notes

- The wiki lives at `wiki.subsurfaces.net`, a subdomain of `subsurfaces.net`
- Articles are written in Markdown with YAML frontmatter
- Wikilinks (`[[Note Title]]`) link between articles
- The site is built with React + Vite and deployed on Cloudflare Workers
