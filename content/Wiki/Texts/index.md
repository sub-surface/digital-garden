## Analytical Architecture

Each text analysis within the Philchat Wiki adheres to a rigorous seven-part analytical format:

1. **Textual Overview & Historical Context**: Publication circumstances, immediate interlocutors, and foundational polemic.
2. **Structural Architecture**: Formal division of the text (parts, books, or numbered propositions).
3. **Core Axioms & Deductions**: Step-by-step reconstruction of central logical moves and demonstrations.
4. **Key Arguments & Landmark Formulations**: The definitive arguments that shifted subsequent philosophy.
5. **Aporias, Paradoxes & Internal Tensions**: Unresolved difficulties or logical friction within the text's system.
6. **Critical Objections & Historical Reception**: Primary counter-arguments from contemporary and later traditions.
7. **Bibliographic Apparatus**: Authoritative critical editions, standard citation pagination, and key secondary literature referenced in the [[Bibliography|Master Bibliography]].
