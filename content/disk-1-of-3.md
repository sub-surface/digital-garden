## What setup would have done

Disk 1 is the one that decides where everything goes. It creates the directory, writes the paths, registers the file associations — all the boring structural work that makes the *later* disks meaningful. It is entirely preparation. Run it alone and you get an empty folder and a promise.

Which is the thing nobody tells you about beginnings: they are the least interesting disk in the set, and losing one is survivable, because a beginning can be reconstructed from what followed it. You can infer setup from the shape of what it installed.

It is the **ending** you cannot reconstruct.
