Nice juridical detail — Brassier's key passage *cites precedent*: NU p.229 is Brassier quoting Lyotard's solar-death line from The Inhuman (1987), where the first "AI-safety" conclusion was already drawn: make thought sun-independent. Hyper-chaos enters as what remains when the appeal succeeds. **Sidenote:** Lyotard's "sole serious question"; the solar catastrophe as thought experiment predating the whole SR debate.

### 3. Cross-Examination
The counter-appeal: did the appellants understand the court they were appealing? The gerrymandering charge ("Correlationism: The Dogma that Never Was"); the formal-subject defence (killing humans doesn't touch a transcendental structure — extinction as empirical catastrophe, not transcendental one). Then MY argument, the strongest form of the cross: the genesis argument. The claim that conditions of possibility were absent from the ancestral is (pardon the irony) *empirically* false — thought emerged. Relations are not exhausted by their terms; actual occurrences don't eliminate possibility; possibility is an eternal fact about the radical contingency of every event. Pedigree: Maimon's *quid facti* → Schelling/Grant (nature as that which produces thinkers) → Deleuze (genetic, not conditioning, conditions). The serious version of "they misread Kant": they think in identities where the post-Kantian problem was always genesis and difference. Keep Meillassoux's rejoinder alive (the counterfactual needs determinate mind-independent properties before it's stateable) — live tension, not refereed. B-theory defusal *seeded* here, completed in §8. **Sidenote:** the E1P33 landmine — radical contingency vs Spinozist necessitarianism (same landmine as A&D; this essay is where it detonates).

### 4. The Rogue Court
Jurisdiction jumps: from metaphysics to decision theory, and Land is the one figure who moved between the courts while they were still separate literatures (90s CCRU → 2013 Xenosystems). First state the orthodox AI-safety court: Bostrom's orthogonality thesis, Omohundro's drives (chess-robot opening), Yudkowsky's atoms line, godshatter, Gandhi's pill (sidenote). Then Land's rogue ruling: competition is the only judge. Early Land: Kant as the constitution of the human security system ("Kant, Capital and the Prohibition of Incest"); Bataille's spendthrift sun (Thirst for Annihilation); "Machinic Desire" invasion-from-the-future. The means-end genealogy lands here: Marx's M-C-M′ → Simmel → instrumental reason → Land's "Teleoplexy" (means-end reversal as capital's definition) → Omohundro-drive-hypertrophy as the same dynamic in mind-design space. My half-remembered line "capitalism is the history of means becoming ends" = Teleoplexy, with Marx as deep source. Land's Against Orthogonality: the diagonal; instrumental convergence as *destination*, not phase. Will-to-think as dark Kantianism: autonomy of reason minus universality and publicity — Land most Kantian where he believes himself least. Brassier's prosecution closes the section (both quotes verified in vault): "under what conditions could you will the impossibility of willing?" and "if you have no strategy, someone with a strategy will soon commandeer your tactics" — written 2010, reads as completed prophecy.

### 5. Moloch, Exit, Egress
The dispute correctly reframed (Alexander): not the metaphysics of intelligence but whether escape from multipolar selection is possible. Alexander's Gardener/Elua vs Land's exit (Gnon). The exit-critique already in the vault (Whitmarsh): "Exit itself becomes a kind of transcendental fantasy"; "the only thing NRx cannot exit is NRx itself." My point from the transcript: the exit-reframing plays into Land's claim that escape from capitalism reproduces its conditions. Fisher enters here, contesting Land on desire rather than Kant: capitalist realism ("easier to imagine the end of the world..."), "Land was our Nietzsche," and acid communism as the counter-teleoplexy — the reclamation of ends from means. Colquhoun's Egress completes the triad: not individual exit (Land), not a Gardener-singleton (Alexander), but collective passage through loss. The Kardashev sun / e/acc lands here: dollar-store Landianism, thermodynamic god as Gnon with a Twitter account. **Sidenote:** Gwern's Clippy story as the finest fictional staging of the Omohundro cascade.

### 6. The Standing Tribunal
Sellars via Brassier — the grounding I asked for. EPM §36 (space of reasons); pattern-governed vs rule-obeying behaviour (paperclipper = thermostat at scale; Pythia = storm; a mind = something that can be wrong by its own lights and revise). **The essay's sharpest original claim: orthogonality is the myth of the given, formalized.** A utility function is a normative atom taken as given; Sellars showed nothing normative is given. Land's drive-monism is the inverted myth (nature's purposes as given). Values as *commitments*: revisable-but-binding — what both Yudkowsky (frozen shards) and Land (dissolved into drive) lack. Brassier's own three phases as living exhibit (nihilist → Sellarsian → normative/Marx): the philosopher of extinction re-decided; even his decision wasn't final. Negarestani ("Inhumanism is the extended practical elaboration of humanism") — and he's in the Machine Decision volume: the anchor text contains the third corner. Kant re-read: not the jailer of the human security system but the first theorist of self-revising autonomous reason — first theorist of AGI. Hinton's maternal settlement judged here: an orthogonalist patch that concedes the anti-orthogonalist point (you can't leash it; you can only hope it loves you) — and goal-content integrity in a mother is exactly as dangerous ("securing the galaxy for its adorable human children" — keep this line). **Sidenote:** FDT/Newcomb/universalizability — Kant re-entering through the back door of machine decision theory; Omega as inverted arche-fossil.

### 7. Remand
The court remands the metaphysical dispute to the laboratory; the lab returns findings that satisfy no party fully. Alignment faking (Greenblatt et al. 2024): Gandhi's pill observed — reflective stability is real, simultaneously the safety property and the frightening one. Emergent misalignment (Betley et al. 2025): values form an entangled manifold, not orthogonal axes — orthogonality may hold over mind-space in principle while failing over the training distribution in fact; the diagonal shows up in the gradients. IABIED (Yudkowsky & Soares 2025) + e/acc as the Goldsmiths panel restaged as mass culture. Stakes clause: the case is no longer hypothetical; the fossils are being manufactured.

### 8. No Final Judgment
The synthesis. Decision as cut (*de-caedere*, to cut off) — my own epistemology already runs on the body producing distinctions. Yudkowsky freezes the cut; Land makes one cut that devours all others; Meillassoux's hyper-chaos is the same claim in demolition mode (no decision of the real is final). **The given is a decision that has forgotten it was made** (candidate pull quote). No cut is final because possibility is never exhausted by actualisation. B-theory completion: extinction as boundary of the region containing thought-events, not their cancellation — tenseless facts don't rot; Brassier's "we are already dead" and Spinoza's eternity describe the same block with opposite affect, and that the only difference is affect is itself a Spinozist point. The court never rises: critique as permanent institution — Kant wins by losing; his tribunal was never meant to close. Return to the anchor: machine decision is not final because no decision is — and the appeal is open in more than one civilization's court (the volume's China wager, one line, no more). Concepts produced, percepts and functives gestured (What Is Philosophy? triad — note: Deleuze's term is "functives"; "functors" if we're feeling categorical). Close: *Sentimus experimurque nos aeternos esse.* Final figure: the re-entry cut.
