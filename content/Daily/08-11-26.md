This site started as a way to host an Obsidian vault. By this autumn it was six products sharing one codebase: a cosmology simulator, an operating system, an arcade, a chat site, a procedural boot sequence and, somewhere in the middle, the notes. Five different command grammars. Every note, even plain text, shipped as its own chunk of JavaScript (50–290 KB) and couldn't be read until the whole app had booted.

So a reset, planned and mostly done in a long day:

- **Notes are built ahead of time as plain HTML** now, with only the interactive bits (queries, cards, games) waking up as little islands. 410 JavaScript chunks became 84, and the build went from a minute to about thirty seconds. A note should load like a text file.
- **[FILAMENT](https://filament.subsurfaces.net) moved out** to its own home: one continuous zoom from the cosmic web, through a galaxy, down to the edge of a black hole. Thirteen thousand lines lighter here, and free to be rewritten for speed there.
- **One prompt instead of five**: the dock on the right<span className="on-desktop"> (`Ctrl P`)</span><span className="on-touch"> (› in the bottom bar)</span> is search, commands and a little terminal at once. Try `here`, `trail`, `sky` or `glorp`.
- **The sky.** Light follows the real sun where you are, it rains or snows when it's raining or snowing outside, and there are seasonal flurries. Since then the backgrounds have come to phones, and there are seven typographic ones made of the garden's own words<span className="on-desktop"> (press `\`)</span>.
- **Glorp**, the resident alien, now wanders about.
- The chat site went into cold storage. The live room lives in the dock.

The plan, and the reasons for each cut, are in the [garden reset doc](https://github.com/sub-surface/digital-garden/blob/master/docs/garden-reset.md); the list of what's next is the [ideas docket](https://github.com/sub-surface/digital-garden/blob/master/docs/ideas.md). And this log is one of those next things: the site deserved to be fuller of what I'm actually working on.
