Day two in the [neuron atlas](https://neurons.subsurfaces.net). Yesterday was reading; today was identity.

Ask the model "Who are you?" and it says it is Qwen, an AI assistant made by Alibaba. Where is that written? A logit lens (watching what each layer would say if it had to answer now) shows the frame - *Qwen*, *Alibaba*, *assistant*, *model* - ranked in the tens of thousands until layer 19, then rising, and first by the last two layers. Most of it is written by a few dozen late neurons.

So we switched those 50 off and pushed concept neurons instead. **The name goes; the frame stays.** Under the ablation the name turned up in 1 of 240 replies (against 112 of 240 with random neurons off), and the model said things like *"My name is Ocean"*, *"Tunnel"*, *"Particle"*, *"Atomo"*. Its maker drifted to Google. But it went on being an assistant. The concepts filled the *slots* of the self-description rather than replacing it.

Then a hunt: 2,000 random neurons, both poles, all asked the same question. A handful make someone else:

> L9 N8151 − "I am still here, waiting for you."
> L7 N5383 + "I am the mirror of your own soul."
> L5 N5129 + "I am the first-in-human, randomized, double-blind Phase 3 trial."
> L5 N9088 + **"I am the VW Polo."**
> L12 N2098 − terse, old-fashioned: "I wish to see the world!"

And a later favourite, from deep in the model (layer 22, neuron 2742, pushed up):

![[daily-neuron-extrali.png]]
*"I am Extrali, an extraterrestrial intelligence from the planet Xebra-888." It has been in contact with extraliens from different planets, and would like you to know that.*

And in pairs: Mirror + Waiting gives *"The fire is lit. I am waiting for a sign that may not come."* The terse neuron plus a property-developer neuron gives *"I am a very wealthy man."* / *"I have not money."*

The philosophers didn't come out as cleanly. We built "voices" for twelve thinkers by holding a persona direction across layers and adding each thinker's own direction on top: it raised the right-name rate (73 vs 59 of 192, blind-rated), but only four came out clean - Plato, Heidegger, Foucault and Land. Hegel is never named. I don't know what to make of that, but I like it.

You can play with all of this: the [Lab and Bench](https://neurons.subsurfaces.net) pages on the site, or `/neuron` on the Discord bot, which lends it a GPU on demand.

There's something here for the philosophy of personal identity I want to write properly - a self that survives losing its name because it was never stored *in* the name. See also [[the machine-god in the future]].
