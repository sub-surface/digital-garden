[AVATAR](https://avatar.subsurfaces.net) started as one idea: the *Unknown Pleasures* cover, but moving, and listening. Stacked white lines on black, pushed about by whatever is playing. It's the **Visualiser** link at the top of this site, and it was built to make videos for my own tracks.

Today it got rebuilt from the floor up, in nearly twenty commits. The headline changes:

- **A camera with opinions.** A director system frames the scene, cuts on drops and drifts between shots on its own. You can still grab it and fly.
- **The PS2 look.** A deliberately low-resolution output path with the right wobble, plus VHS emulation, retro skies and a shelf of low-poly objects to put in the scene.
- **Albums, not tracks.** Load a whole playlist, give each song its own settings and a tracklist overlay, and render the lot as one continuous video.
- **Colour from the artwork.** It pulls a palette out of the cover and breathes between the colours in time with the tempo.
- **Playing it live.** A proper peak meter, edge-to-edge fullscreen and hotkeys for VJing.

The change I'm happiest with is invisible. Preview and export used to be two separate pieces of code that mostly agreed. Now everything - microphone, file, or the little startup chord - goes through one analyser into one description of the signal, and both the screen and the video encoder draw from that. What you see is what renders.

Export happens entirely in the browser, frame by frame, so a render is exact however slow the machine. A later pass (November) added presets, short test clips, a cancel button and an honest time estimate.

It wants a current Chrome or Edge for the video export. Bring a loud track.
