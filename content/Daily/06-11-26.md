The cute one. **[numogram.subsurfaces.net](https://numogram.subsurfaces.net)** is a website after the [Ccru](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cybernetic_Culture_Research_Unit), the 1990s Warwick collective of Nick Land, Sadie Plant, Mark Fisher and friends, and their **Numogram**: the digits 0 to 9 paired off into five "syzygies" that sum to nine, with currents between them, drawn as a map of a decimal labyrinth. [[the noumenon has fangs]] was my way into Land; the doodles and the glyph come from the back pages of his collected writings, *Fanged Noumena* (pp. 648–657).

![[daily-numogram.png]]

The Numogram *is* the navigation. Every zone is a room:

0. **Transmissions** - a gallery of dithered plates, tapestries and animations (below).
1. **Anglossic Qabbala** - a live AQ calculator (A = 10 … Z = 35; hover any word on the site and the cursor reads its number) and a lexicon of coincidences.
2. **Tic Xenotation** - Land's prime-factor notation, drawn live.
3. **Solar Economy** - a dithered sun on two clocks: [Bataille](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Accursed_Share)'s expenditure clock against Brassier's extinction clock.
4. **Holey Space** - drag to bore through Negarestani's crust; worm tunnels keep eating it.
5. **Spinal Descent** - scroll down the "Spinal Catastrophism" doodle, neck to tailbone.
6. **Warp Arcade** - six games in a fake "Ccru Flash Player 5" whose preloader really loads and still stalls at 81%: Lemurian Time War, Cyclonopedia Driller, Spinal Catastrophe, Solar Expenditure, Garbage Time and Flatline.
7. **Glyphling** - the glyph from page 648 as a little pet. Feed it words.
8. **Library** - the doodle pages, sources, colophon.
9. **Webring** - 88×31 badges, Numogram Radio, a closed guestbook.

![[daily-numogram-plate02.png]]
*Plate 02, Solar Economy. "Why does the sun take so long to die?"*

The rules are computed, not imitated: the Xenotation follows the "Xenotation-1" page, the gates are digital roots of triangular numbers, and every quote is checked against the books. Every sound effect and all six soundtracks are synthesised from scratch in a small numpy synth, no samples, the loops tuned to E at about 81 Hz because that's what SUN adds up to in AQ.

Zero build, vanilla JavaScript, and the source is public at [sub-surface/numogram](https://github.com/sub-surface/numogram). Turn the sound on.
