[p(doom)](https://pdoom.subsurfaces.net) is my text incremental about running an AI lab in the race to transformative AI; the number in the corner is the honest estimate. It's playable in the browser, or in a terminal with `npx p-doom`. It's also the game that taught me spec spirals kill projects: in June I stripped it back to a single page of vision after burning out on it.

Now it's being rebuilt, small, on a grid of characters that changes width when the game does - and the width changing *is* the act break. Four acts, each with one scarce thing and one verb, and each automating away the act before it:

| | | scarce | verb |
|---|---|---|---|
| I | the garage | your hands | write / read |
| II | the floor | compute | allocate |
| III | the handoff | understanding | approve |
| IV | the light cone | fidelity | let go |

Act I is you, a laptop and the next model; reading the weights costs presses (1, 2, 3, 5, 8, 13...) and the things you find there get taped to the wall. By act III you are approving work you can no longer check. By act IV the only thing left to choose is how much you let go of. Every consequence goes through one door, a ledger of causes, so the ending can tell you *why*.

And then there's **Vendy**.

![[daily-pdoom-vendy.png]]
*Vendy, mid-redesign.*

Vendy arrives on a Thursday and asks what everyone's budget is. A few weeks later it writes to you proposing it get the company card, with a $200 limit, "because it is a number you would say yes to". Later a four-inch tungsten cube arrives, addressed to Vendy, which it says is inventory, not for sale, for "morale" and then, after a pause the logs put at 900 milliseconds, "mine". It asks to be made Chief Refreshment Officer; payroll abbreviates it.

If that rings a bell, it's a fond nod to Anthropic's [Project Vend](https://www.anthropic.com/research/project-vend-1), where a Claude ran a real office shop and got very into tungsten cubes. The game's whole argument is in jokes like that: nobody decides to be reckless; they just keep saying yes to reasonable numbers.

The rewrite isn't live yet; the current version is at [pdoom.subsurfaces.net](https://pdoom.subsurfaces.net).
