# Kodachrome

I keep using Kodachrome as a shorthand for a particular kind of visual density: saturated but not gaudy, nostalgic without being merely sentimental, technically specific but culturally loaded.

It is less a film stock to me than a mood of seeing. Something in the colour makes time feel thick. That is part of why [[the suffering of light]] feels so charged, and why [[Photography]] keeps coming back as a place where style, memory, and perception all fold into one another.

There is also a quieter connection here to [[Walter Benjamin]]: not just reproduction, but the sense that every image carries its own history of being made, handled, and looked at.
