# Philosophy of Mind

This is one of those umbrella terms that is too broad to be satisfying and still somehow necessary. I use it as a place to stash questions about consciousness, attention, embodiment, language, and whatever else refuses to stay neatly inside the skull.

The note I want to keep making is that mind is not just private interiority. [[On Attention]] points toward that already, and so does the feeling that thought is always happening in some medium, through some practice, with some world pressing back.

It also keeps pulling me toward [[Wittgenstein]], [[Gilles Deleuze]], and more embodied accounts of subjectivity. I do not want the mind to become a ghost-story. I want it to stay messy, situated, and alive.
