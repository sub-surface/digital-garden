# Walter Benjamin

Benjamin feels useful to me less as a sealed-off theorist than as a way of reading the afterlife of things: photographs, commodities, citations, ruins, fragments of culture that keep moving after the world that made them has already changed.

What I keep coming back to is the sense that aura is not just a property of art objects. It can attach to an image, a scene, a voice, even a memory of looking. That makes him a useful companion for thinking about [[Photography]], [[On Photography]], and the strange half-life of images online.

He also sits close to the way I think about writing itself: not as a smooth line of argument, but as montage, as excerpt, as accretion. That feels adjacent to [[On Diagrams]] and to the habit of collecting partial thoughts until they start to speak to each other.
