# Gilles Deleuze

Deleuze is one of those thinkers who keeps reappearing in my notes because he gives language to things I already half-suspect: becoming over identity, relation over essence, movement over static description.

I reach for him whenever a note wants to become a map instead of a definition. That is part of why he keeps linking back to [[On Diagrams]] and to the more experimental side of writing, where the point is not to close meaning down but to let it branch.

I also think he changes how [[Philosophy of Mind]] feels from the inside. The mind stops looking like a sealed container and starts looking like an arrangement of forces, habits, thresholds, and encounters.
