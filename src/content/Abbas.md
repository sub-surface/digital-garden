# Abbas

Abbas gives me a way to think about photographs as witnesses without making them inert. The image can be formally beautiful and still carry social pressure, history, weather, faith, and vulnerability all at once.

That makes him feel adjacent to [[Thomas Sauvin]] and to [[On the discarded]], but also to the more general question of how images hold onto the world. I keep wanting to read photography as both surface and evidence.

He also fits into the larger ecology of visual thought in this vault: [[Walter Benjamin]], [[Kodachrome]], [[Photography]], and the ongoing attempt to name why certain images stay with me longer than they should.
