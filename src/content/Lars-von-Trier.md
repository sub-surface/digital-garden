# Lars von Trier

What interests me about von Trier is not the provocation for its own sake, but the way he turns constraint into a generator. The form is often abrasive, but the abrasion is doing work. It forces the question of what happens when a filmmaker cannot rely on comfort, polish, or default habits.

[[The Five Obstructions]] is the obvious entry point, but I read it more broadly as a note about process: limitations can expose a practice to itself. That is why he keeps connecting, for me, to [[On starting]] and to any writing or image-making session that needs a shove to get moving.

He is also a useful counterpoint to more contemplative cinema. Where some directors invite drift, von Trier creates friction. That friction can be irritating, but it can also produce real thought.
