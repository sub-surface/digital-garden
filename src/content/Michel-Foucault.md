# Michel Foucault

Foucault keeps showing up whenever I start paying attention to the hidden scaffolding of an experience. The question is rarely “what is this thing?” and more often “what are the rules, histories, and disciplines that make it show up as this thing at all?”

That makes him useful for reading images, institutions, and even habits of self-presentation. I hear him in [[Action & Voice]], in the social life of images, and in any note where power is not a theme but a background condition.

He also has a very strong pull toward the archive and the diagrammatic. Not because he loves abstraction, but because he knows that systems become visible when you look at their arrangements rather than their slogans.
